Third-party risk management often starts with a questionnaire.

But a questionnaire is not a risk program.

It is only one input into a much larger operating model: supplier inventory, criticality, ownership, evidence, issue tracking, escalation, and continuous governance.

The real question is not whether a supplier completed a form.

The real question is whether the organization understands what it depends on, what can go wrong, and who owns the decision when risk is material.

I put together a practical guide on moving TPRM from checkbox compliance to a program that actually supports business decisions.
